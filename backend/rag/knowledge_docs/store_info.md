# Store Information & Human Support

## Customer Support Hours
Our human customer support team operates Monday through Saturday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM PKT.
Inquiries received outside of operational hours are queued and addressed on the next working morning.

## Human Escalation & Complex Disputes
If an automated voice agent cannot resolve an issue, customers are routed to a human specialist.
You can request human assistance during the call by asking "I want to speak to a representative" or "transfer to agent".
Support emails: support@ourbrand.com
Direct WhatsApp helpline: +92 300 0000000 (operational during business hours).
