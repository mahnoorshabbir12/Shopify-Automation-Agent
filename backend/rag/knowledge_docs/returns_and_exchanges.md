# Returns, Refunds, and Exchange Policy

## 7-Day Return Window
Customers may request a return or exchange within 7 days (7 calendar days) from the parcel delivery date.

## Eligibility Criteria
- The item must be completely unused, unwashed, and in the same pristine condition as received.
- Original product tags, protective seals, and brand packaging must remain intact.
- Items purchased on clearance or final sale cannot be exchanged or refunded.

## Defective or Damaged Parcels
If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item:
- Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.
- We arrange reverse courier pickup at zero cost to the customer and issue an immediate replacement or full refund.
