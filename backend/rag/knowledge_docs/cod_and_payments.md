# Cash on Delivery (COD) & Payment Policies

## Payment Method
We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan. Customers pay with physical cash directly to the courier rider upon receiving the package.

## Verification & Rider Rules
- Courier riders cannot open the parcel or hand over products before receiving full payment.
- Please have exact cash ready at the time of delivery to avoid courier delay.
- If a customer refuses to accept the parcel without a valid reason, their COD privileges on future orders may be restricted.

## Advance Payment
Orders above PKR 15,000 or custom customized products may require a 10% advance deposit via Bank Transfer, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa before dispatch.
