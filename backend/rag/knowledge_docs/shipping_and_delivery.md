# Shipping & Delivery Guidelines

## Delivery Timelines
- Major Cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad): 2 to 4 business days.
- Regional & Other Cities across Pakistan: 3 to 6 business days.
- During high-demand sale seasons (e.g. Blessed Friday, Eid), deliveries may take an additional 1 to 2 business days.

## Courier Partners
All shipments are booked and tracked through verified logistics partners:
- TCS Courier
- PostEx
- BlueEX

## Shipping Charges
- Flat rate delivery fee is PKR 200 nationwide.
- Orders with subtotal exceeding PKR 3,000 qualify for 100% Free Shipping.
